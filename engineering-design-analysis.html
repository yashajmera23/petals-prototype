Engineering Design & Analysis

Engineering calculations, design development, analysis and technical review.

Design that starts from the ground data. We analyse and design structures, roads and site works, and review designs prepared by others, with calculations and drawings the whole project team can follow.

What we cover

15 parameters in 4 groups. Select any item to see what it involves. Specialist tests are delivered through PETALS' technical network and qualified partners where required.

5

Civil & Structural Engineering

Analysis and design of building and civil structures.

  • Structural Analysis

    Models a structure under its design loads to determine forces, moments and deflections in each member.

  • RCC Design

    Designs reinforced concrete members such as slabs, beams, columns and foundations to the applicable Indian Standards.

  • Steel Structure Design

    Designs steel members and connections for sheds, frames and supporting structures.

  • Foundation Design Coordination

    Coordinates geotechnical recommendations, subsurface conditions and structural loading requirements to support appropriate foundation design. The extent of geotechnical and structural design responsibility is defined according to the project scope.

  • Load Calculations

    Determines dead, live, wind and seismic loads for the structure according to the applicable codes.

3

Roads & Pavement Engineering

Road alignments and the pavements that carry the traffic.

  • Road Geometric Design

    Designs road alignment, gradients, curves and cross-sections for safe and efficient traffic movement.

  • Pavement Design

    Designs flexible and rigid pavement layers for the expected traffic and subgrade strength, following IRC guidelines.

  • Pavement Evaluation

    Interprets pavement investigation and evaluation results to develop recommendations for overlay design, strengthening, rehabilitation or reconstruction. Field surveys and structural pavement testing are covered under Pavement & Infrastructure Investigation.

3

Drainage & Site Development

Shaping the site and managing the water on it.

  • Stormwater Drainage Design

    Sizes drains, culverts and outfalls to carry rainfall runoff away from the site and structures.

  • Grading & Site Development

    Plans finished levels, slopes and platforms for buildings, roads and drainage.

  • Earthwork Quantities

    Calculates cut and fill volumes from survey and design levels for planning and costing.

4

Analysis & Design Review

Checking the numbers and documenting the design.

  • Engineering Calculations

    Prepares and documents design calculations for review, approval and construction.

  • Design Review

    Reviews designs prepared by others for compliance with applicable requirements, safety, constructability and technical adequacy. The term “independent review” should be used only where the review is separately commissioned and genuinely independent of the original design.

  • Value Engineering

    Evaluates alternative design, material and construction solutions to optimise cost, time and constructability while maintaining specified safety, functionality, durability and applicable code requirements.

  • Technical Drawings & Documentation

    Prepares drawings, specifications and reports that communicate the design clearly.
