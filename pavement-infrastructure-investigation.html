Pavement & Infrastructure Investigation

Pavement and infrastructure investigation, condition assessment and specialised testing for highways, roads, bridges and tunnels.

Roads, bridges and tunnels are assessed while they stay in service. Survey vehicles, deflectometers and structural monitoring show how the asset is performing, so maintenance and strengthening go where they are needed.

Pavement Investigation & Structural Evaluation

Surveying the road network and testing how each pavement layer carries load.

  • Network Survey Vehicle (NSV – 2D & 3D)

    Network-level road surveys using vehicle-mounted imaging and measurement systems to capture pavement condition, road geometry and associated road assets.

  • Falling Weight Deflectometer (FWD)

    Applies an impulse load to the pavement and measures the resulting deflection basin to evaluate structural response and support pavement assessment.

  • Heavy Weight Deflectometer (HWD)

    Applies higher impulse loads to assess pavement structural response, particularly for heavily loaded pavement and airfield applications.

  • Laser Dynamic Deflectometer (LDD)

    Uses laser-based dynamic measurements to obtain continuous pavement deflection information while the survey vehicle moves along the road.

  • Lightweight Deflectometer (LWD)

    Evaluates the deformation response and stiffness characteristics of pavement layers, prepared subgrades and compacted materials under dynamic loading.

  • Benkelman Beam Deflection Test

    Measures pavement rebound deflection under a controlled wheel load to assess the structural condition of flexible pavements.

  • Surface Roughness Survey (Bump Integrator)

    Measures longitudinal road surface roughness using vehicle-mounted equipment and derives relevant pavement roughness indicators.

  • Pavement Condition Assessment

    Records and rates visible pavement surface distress to support maintenance planning and structural evaluation.

  • Pavement Skid Resistance Testing

    Evaluates pavement surface friction characteristics to assess resistance to vehicle skidding under specified test conditions.

  • Pavement Material Investigation

    Investigates pavement layer composition, thickness and material characteristics through suitable field investigation, sampling and laboratory evaluation.

  • Cement Concrete (CC) & Bituminous Concrete (BC) Core Cutting

    Extracts pavement cores for thickness measurement and material evaluation, with reinforcement location using a rebar locator for cement concrete pavement where required.

  • Resilient Modulus Testing of Bituminous Mixtures

    Determines the resilient stiffness characteristics of bituminous mixtures under repeated loading using applicable IRC guidance and ASTM test procedures.

Road Safety, Traffic & Highway Surveys

How safely the road works, how well it is lit and what traffic it carries.

  • Road Safety Audit

    Assesses highway and road infrastructure to identify potential safety deficiencies relating to road geometry, intersections, signs, markings, barriers, visibility and road-user safety, with recommendations for improvement.

  • Environmental Audit

    Reviews environmental aspects of infrastructure projects within the agreed audit scope, identifying potential environmental concerns relating to air quality, noise, drainage, waste management and environmental protection measures.

  • Retroreflectometer Testing (Road Markings & Signages)

    Measures the retroreflective performance of road markings and traffic signs to assess their visibility, particularly under nighttime driving conditions.

  • Illuminance (Lux) Measurement

    Measures illumination levels using a lux meter to assess lighting adequacy and distribution across roads and infrastructure facilities.

  • Axle Load Survey

    Records vehicle axle-load distribution to assess traffic loading patterns and support pavement design and evaluation.

  • Traffic Volume Count (TVC)

    Records and classifies traffic volume over specified periods to support traffic analysis, highway planning and pavement design.

  • Roadometer / Distance Measurement Survey

    Measures road distance and chainage using a calibrated measuring wheel to support field surveys and road measurements.

Bridge Investigation & Structural Assessment

Inspecting, load testing and monitoring bridges in service.

  • Mobile Bridge Inspection Unit (MBIU)

    Provides mobile platform access to difficult-to-reach bridge components, particularly the underside of bridge decks, for inspection and condition assessment.

  • Bridge Load Testing

    Evaluates bridge structural response under specified test loading through measurements of deflection, strain and other relevant parameters.

  • Bridge Health Monitoring System (BHMS)

    Provides an integrated bridge monitoring arrangement using selected structural sensors, data acquisition and reporting systems to monitor defined response parameters over time. Monitoring parameters, frequency, alert criteria and reporting requirements are established for each project.

  • Wireless Structural Health Monitoring

    Uses wireless sensor networks for remote acquisition and transmission of structural response data from bridges and other infrastructure. This may form part of an overall Bridge Health Monitoring System or be delivered as a separately specified monitoring arrangement.

  • Bridge Bearing Inspection & Condition Assessment (Elastomeric & POT-PTFE)

    Assesses the condition of installed elastomeric and pot-PTFE bridge bearings through site inspection, identifying visible damage, deformation, displacement, misalignment, deterioration and other signs of distress.

  • Bridge Vibration Analysis

    Evaluates the dynamic response of bridge structures through vibration measurements and analysis of relevant modal characteristics.

Tunnel Investigation

Recording the condition of tunnel linings.

  • Tunnel Defect Detection System (TDDS)

    Uses specialised tunnel inspection and detection systems to identify, record and document visible tunnel defects and lining irregularities for condition assessment.
