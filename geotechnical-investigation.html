Geotechnical Investigation

Subsurface investigation, field testing, soil and rock evaluation, and geotechnical engineering and design.

Every foundation decision starts with the ground. We plan each investigation around the structure and the site, test and log what is there, and turn the results into clear design recommendations.

Field Investigation & Testing

Tests carried out on site, in boreholes, pits and at foundation level.

  • Borehole Drilling

    Drilled boreholes that recover soil and rock samples and record the ground profile with depth.

  • Standard Penetration Test (SPT)

    Counts the blows needed to drive a split-spoon sampler, giving a measure of soil density and strength at each depth.

  • Cone Penetration Test (CPT/CPTu)

    A cone pushed at a steady rate gives a continuous profile of soil resistance and, with CPTu, pore water pressure.

  • Dynamic Cone Penetration Test (DCPT)

    A driven cone measures penetration resistance quickly, often for shallow strata and pavement subgrades.

  • Trial Pits / Test Pits

    Shallow excavations to see the soil layers directly, take samples and check for groundwater.

  • Field Vane Shear Test

    Measures the undrained shear strength of soft clays in place, without disturbing them.

  • Plate Load Test

    Loads a steel plate at foundation level to measure bearing capacity and settlement under load.

  • Field CBR Test

    Measures the California Bearing Ratio of the subgrade in place, for pavement design.

  • In-situ Permeability Testing

    Measures how readily water flows through the soil or rock, in boreholes or pits.

  • Pressuremeter Testing

    Expands a probe against the borehole wall to measure the stiffness and strength of the ground.

6

Sampling, Logging & Groundwater

What is recovered from the ground, how it is recorded, and what the water table is doing.

  • Disturbed Soil Sampling

    Samples for classification and index tests, where the soil structure does not need to be preserved.

  • Undisturbed Soil Sampling

    Tube samples that keep the soil structure and moisture for strength and consolidation testing.

  • Rock Coring & Core Logging

    Recovering rock cores and logging rock type, weathering, fractures and core recovery.

  • Borehole Logging & Strata Profiling

    Recording each layer encountered to build the subsurface profile across the site.

  • Rock Quality Designation (RQD)

    A measure of rock quality: the share of sound core pieces longer than 100 mm in each run.

  • Groundwater Level Observation

    Recording groundwater levels during and after drilling, for design and construction planning.

10

Geotechnical Engineering & Design

Turning the investigation into foundation, slope and retaining-structure decisions.

  • Geotechnical Design Recommendations

    Foundation type, depth and design parameters drawn directly from the investigation results.

  • Bearing Capacity & Settlement Analysis

    Safe bearing pressure and expected settlement for the proposed foundations.

  • Shallow Foundation Design

    Design of isolated, combined and raft foundations to suit the ground conditions.

  • Pile Foundation Design Support

    Pile type, length and capacity estimates to support the structural design.

  • Earth Pressure & Retaining Structures

    Lateral earth pressure assessment for retaining walls, basements and abutments.

  • Slope & Embankment Stability

    Stability analysis of natural slopes, cuttings and embankments.

  • Ground Improvement Assessment

    Whether ground improvement is needed, and which methods suit the site and the structure.

  • Excavation Stability Assessment

    Stability and support needs of excavations, and their effect on neighbouring ground.

  • Liquefaction Assessment

    The risk of loose, saturated soils losing strength during an earthquake.

  • Soil–Structure Interaction

    How the ground and the structure respond together under load.
