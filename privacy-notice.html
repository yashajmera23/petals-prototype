1Who we are

This notice explains how PETALS Engineering Consultancy (“PETALS”, “we”, “us”), Jaipur, Rajasthan, India, handles personal data collected through this website. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, PETALS is the Data Fiduciary for that data.

You can contact us about this notice at [Official Email].

In plain words. This is us: an engineering firm in Jaipur. Questions go to one email address.

2What we collect

We collect only the details you choose to send us:

  • Project enquiries: your name, organisation, email address, phone number, project location, the services you are interested in and a description of your project.
  • Job applications: your name, contact details, the role you apply for, your CV and any cover note.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider records basic server logs (IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the site secure and working.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data, and we ask you not to include it in your messages.

In plain words. Only what you type into our forms, plus the basic logs every website keeps.

3How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal, consider your application for current and future roles, and keep this website secure. We do not use your details for automated decision-making, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

In plain words. To answer you. Nothing else.

4Why we are allowed to use it

We process personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you submit a form, and for legitimate uses permitted by law, such as keeping records we are required to keep. You can withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it.

In plain words. You agree when you press send, and you can change your mind.

5Who we share it with

We never sell or rent personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the website and our email, under contracts that require them to protect it, and with authorities where the law requires us to.

Some providers may store data outside India. Where they do, we make sure the transfer is permitted under Indian law.

In plain words. Never sold. Only our website and email providers see it.

6How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for:

DataKept for
Project enquiries that do not lead to work24 months
Enquiries that become projectsFor the project, then as required by law
Job applications12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner
Server logs90 days

In plain words. Enquiries for two years, applications for one, logs for three months.

7Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add visitor statistics in the future, we will update this notice and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

In plain words. No tracking cookies.

8Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to erase it;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

In plain words. See it, fix it, delete it, or complain about it.

9How we protect it

We use reasonable security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to inboxes and files, and providers with recognised security practices. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send confidential drawings through the website form; we will share a secure upload link instead.

In plain words. Encrypted, restricted, and no drawings through the form.

10Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services or the law change. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

In plain words. If it changes, the date at the top changes.
