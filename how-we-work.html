Seven Stages of Project Delivery

Each stage has defined activities and outputs. Depending on project sequencing and technical dependencies, activities may overlap or proceed in parallel. The drawings follow one illustrative project, a building site with 80 proposed boreholes, from the first review to the final handover.

  1. 1

    Stage 01 · Project requirements

    Understand

    Review project objectives, site conditions, available drawings and client requirements to understand the investigation and engineering needs.

  2. 2

    Stage 02 · Services and deliverables

    Define the Scope

    Identify services, investigation quantities, testing requirements, design inputs and deliverables.

  3. 3

    Stage 03 · Programme and resources

    Plan, Schedule & Mobilise

    Develop the project programme, sequence activities, allocate resources and equipment, coordinate site access and establish milestones for investigation, testing, design and delivery.

    Survey and utility clearance come before drilling; geophysics runs alongside it, laboratory testing follows the samples, and design is staged with BIM alongside.

  4. 4

    Stage 04 · Field and laboratory work

    Execute

    Execute the approved programme through site surveys, utility detection, geotechnical investigation, geophysical surveys, field testing, sampling and laboratory testing in accordance with agreed methods and specifications.

  5. 5

    Stage 05 · Technical validation

    Review & Interpret

    Verify field records, borehole logs, geophysical data and laboratory results; interpret ground conditions, establish relevant engineering parameters and resolve technical discrepancies through quality review.

  6. 6

    Stage 06 · Analysis and BIM

    Engineer & Design

    Translate validated investigation and testing results into engineering recommendations, foundation design, analysis and coordinated digital models, in accordance with project requirements.

    The extent of design and BIM is agreed for each project. Not every project needs every discipline.

  7. 7

    Stage 07 · Final project outputs

    Deliver & Communicate

    Submit quality-reviewed project deliverables, communicate key findings and recommendations, and provide the agreed technical documentation, designs and digital outputs.

Two connected workstreams

Within the seven stages, the technical work runs in up to two connected streams. Not every project needs both: the agreed scope decides. Try each scope to see how a project uses them.

Working principles

Four commitments that apply at every stage, on every project.

  • Technical Accuracy

    Appropriate methods, reliable records and careful review.

  • Clear Communication

    Defined expectations, progress updates and prompt clarification.

  • Planned Delivery

    Timelines aligned with scope, access and resource availability.

  • Defined Deliverables

    Outputs agreed at the outset and prepared for their intended use.
