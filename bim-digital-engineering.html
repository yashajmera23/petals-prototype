BIM & Digital Engineering

Digital modelling, design coordination and integrated engineering workflows.

One shared model for the whole project. We model, coordinate and check designs digitally, link the model to programme and cost, and hand over information that stays useful after construction.

What we cover

18 parameters in 4 groups. Select any item to see what it involves. Specialist tests are delivered through PETALS' technical network and qualified partners where required.

5

Building Information Modelling

Three-dimensional models for each discipline.

  • 3D BIM Modelling

    Builds intelligent 3D models of buildings and infrastructure that carry geometry and data together.

  • Architectural BIM

    Models walls, floors, openings and finishes to represent the architectural design.

  • Structural BIM

    Models the structural frame, foundations and reinforcement for coordination and documentation.

  • Civil & Infrastructure BIM

    Models roads, drainage, earthworks and utilities for infrastructure projects.

  • MEP BIM Coordination

    Models and coordinates mechanical, electrical and plumbing services with the structure and architecture.

4

Model Coordination & Integration

Bringing the disciplines together before anything is built.

  • Multidisciplinary Model Coordination

    Combines models from each discipline into one federated model to coordinate the design.

  • Clash Detection

    Finds conflicts between elements of different disciplines before they reach site.

  • Integrated Design Coordination

    Manages design information across disciplines so that changes are reflected consistently.

  • Design Review & Model Validation

    Checks models for completeness, accuracy and compliance with project requirements.

4

4D, 5D & Quantity Management

Linking the model to time and cost.

  • 4D BIM (Construction Sequencing)

    Links model elements to the construction programme to visualise and test the build sequence.

  • 5D BIM (Cost Integration)

    Links model quantities to cost data to support estimating and cost control.

  • Quantity Take-Off

    Extracts quantities of materials and elements directly from the model.

  • Model-Based Progress Visualisation

    Uses the model to show construction progress against the plan.

5

Digital Information & Deliverables

Standards, existing conditions and handover information.

  • BIM Execution Planning Support

    Helps define the BIM goals, standards, responsibilities and deliverables for a project.

  • Scan-to-BIM

    Converts laser scan point clouds of existing buildings and sites into accurate models.

  • As-Built BIM Modelling

    Updates the model to reflect what was actually built, for handover and facility management.

  • IFC & OpenBIM Exchange

    Exchanges models in open formats so that different software and teams can share information.

  • Model Documentation & Visualisation

    Produces drawings, schedules and visuals from the model for review and communication.
